Rental agreement summary

  1. Only the renter and listed drivers may drive. Every driver shows a passport or official ID and license at pickup.
  2. The vehicle stays within Yucatán, Quintana Roo and Campeche unless Jaguar Rentals approves otherwise in writing.
  3. No rideshare or paid transport, no off road or beach driving, no smoking or vaping, and no driving under the influence.
  4. The car is delivered full and must be returned full with regular gasoline. Extra fees apply for refueling ($90 USD plus fuel), late return ($15 USD per hour), kilometers over 300 per day ($0.15 USD per km), heavy cleaning ($50 USD), smoking ($150 USD) and lost keys ($300 USD).
  5. The renter pays for any damage, loss or fines during the rental. The vehicle carries its owner's auto policy; this rental does not include coverage purchased for the renter, and payment of any claim is not guaranteed.
  6. In any accident, call Jaguar Rentals at 999 268 2268 right away and give police and insurers complete, truthful information.
  7. A booking deposit of 30% of the rental total (minimum one day's rate) reserves the vehicle. It is nonrefundable and is applied to the rental total. The balance is due before pickup.
  8. The refundable security deposit is due at pickup and returned within 3 business days, minus any charges.
  9. If you cancel, the booking deposit is not refunded. Any balance already paid is refunded in full 72 hours or more before pickup, 50% within 72 hours, and not refunded for no shows.

Your full rental agreement will be sent to you to sign before pickup. It includes these terms in detail and the vehicle condition report.
